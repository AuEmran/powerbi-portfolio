### 1. [Laptop Asset Lifecycle & Warranty Management](dashboards/laptop-asset-lifecycle)
Tracks warranty status, replacement cost exposure, and onboarding/replacement request volume across a large enterprise laptop fleet.
**Skills:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query, data modelling, IT asset analytics, executive reporting.

### 2. [Mobile Fleet Management](dashboards/mobile-fleet-management)
Monitors mobile service usage, cost by carrier, and device registration compliance (ABM/KNOX) across an enterprise mobile fleet.
**Skills:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query, cost analysis, compliance reporting.

### 3. [Business Case & Mediation Mapping (LGA)](dashboards/business-case-mapping)
Geographic (choropleth) view of case and mediation-application volumes by NSW Local Government Area, benchmarked against the total business population per LGA.
**Skills:** Power BI, DAX, geographic (LGA) data modelling, trend analysis.

### 4. [Business Risk Mapping — Bushfire & Flood Prone Businesses (ABN)](dashboards/flood-prone-business-mapping)
Maps registered businesses (by ABN) located in bushfire- and flood-prone areas of NSW, filterable by LGA and industry classification, to support risk-based outreach.
**Skills:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query, geospatial data (LEP flood/bushfire layers), industry classification analysis.
